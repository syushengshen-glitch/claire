Synthesia review: what it is best for

AI avatar video platform for training, explainers, and business communication.

AvatarsTrainingBusiness
Quick verdict: Companies producing repeatable instructional or corporate videos. Paid plans; free demo may be available.

What this tool does well

  • Fast avatar video production
  • Multilingual templates
  • Enterprise collaboration features

Limits to know before paying

  • Subscription cost can be high for occasional use
  • Avatar videos need strong scripts
  • Not suited to every creative format

Where it fits in a workflow

Synthesia is most useful when the surrounding process is already clear. Define the input, the expected output, and the review step before adding it to a team workflow. A tool that feels impressive in a demo but adds another approval loop is usually not worth the subscription.

Start with one recurring task. Measure time saved, correction effort, and the cost of the plan you would actually need. If those three numbers do not improve, keep the free tier or test a narrower alternative.

Alternatives to consider

  • Runway — Creators exploring AI video generation, editing, and visual effects.
  • Descript — Podcasters, educators, and marketers who edit spoken-word content.
  • ElevenLabs — Creators and teams producing narration, dubbing, or accessible audio.

Pricing and availability

Paid plans; free demo may be available. Product availability, language support, and commercial terms can change by country and plan. Confirm the current details on the vendor's site before purchasing.
