Make review: what it is best for

Visual automation builder for complex workflows and multi-step integrations.

Free tierAutomationVisual
Quick verdict: Operators who want more visual control than a simple trigger-action tool. Free tier available; paid plans vary.

What this tool does well

  • Visual scenario builder
  • Handles complex branching
  • Broad app integrations

Limits to know before paying

  • Steeper learning curve
  • Operations-based pricing
  • Errors need operational monitoring

Where it fits in a workflow

Make is most useful when the surrounding process is already clear. Define the input, the expected output, and the review step before adding it to a team workflow. A tool that feels impressive in a demo but adds another approval loop is usually not worth the subscription.

Start with one recurring task. Measure time saved, correction effort, and the cost of the plan you would actually need. If those three numbers do not improve, keep the free tier or test a narrower alternative.

Alternatives to consider

  • Notion AI — Teams that already organize work in Notion and want AI close to their knowledge base.
  • ClickUp AI — Teams already using ClickUp for project operations.
  • Zapier AI — Small teams that need no-code automation across business tools.

Pricing and availability

Free tier available; paid plans vary. Product availability, language support, and commercial terms can change by country and plan. Confirm the current details on the vendor's site before purchasing.
