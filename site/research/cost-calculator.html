AI tool cost calculator

Compare the visible subscription price with the hidden cost of correction time. The calculation runs in your browser and does not send your numbers anywhere.

Use it with real work: enter one recurring task, the minutes it takes before correction, and the minutes you spend fixing the output. The calculator turns those assumptions into a monthly comparison, not a prediction.

Enter your numbers and calculate.

A clearly labelled example

Suppose a task takes 25 minutes, the free plan adds 20 minutes of correction, the paid plan adds 7 minutes, the loaded hourly cost is $35, and the subscription is $20 per seat. At 20 tasks a month, the paid plan is worth testing only if the saved correction time and the subscription still leave a real surplus. The table below is an illustration, not a claim about any vendor.

DifferenceSaved 4.3 hours$131 lower estimated cost

What this calculator cannot know

  • Your hourly cost may be a salary estimate, an opportunity cost, or a billing rate; choose one and state it.
  • Subscription prices, seat rules, credits, taxes, and overages change. Verify them on the vendor's official pricing page.
  • Correction time is variable and should be measured on your own task, not copied from a demo.
  • Privacy, quality, accessibility, and customer risk are not included in the financial result.

A safer decision rule

  1. Run the free plan on one real, non-sensitive task.
  2. Record the first usable result and every minute spent correcting it.
  3. Repeat with the same input and scoring rule after a paid trial.
  4. Keep the paid plan only when the saved correction time is repeatable and the total cost is lower after review.

Formula used

Free monthly effort
tasks × (base minutes + free correction minutes) ÷ 60 × hourly cost

Paid monthly total
subscription × seats + tasks × (base minutes + paid correction minutes) ÷ 60 × hourly cost

Privacy

The calculator is client-side only. No values are stored or transmitted by SignalShelf.
