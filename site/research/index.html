What counts as a first-hand test here

A test must identify the task, tool version or interface, date, exact input, scoring rule, observable result, and limitations. Screenshots or raw output should exist when the claim depends on the interface. A model changing between runs is not a reason to invent a stable winner.

What does not count

Reading a vendor page is not a hands-on test. Asking a model to summarize a product is not a test. Copying a competitor's conclusion is not original evidence. These activities can support research, but they must be labelled for what they are.

Corrections

If a product fact, source, date, or test result is wrong, send the page URL and a primary source through the contact page. Material corrections will update the page and its date.
