How to interpret the score

A total below 14 means the tool has not earned a subscription on this task. A score from 14 to 21 means the result needs more evidence or a narrower workflow. A score above 21 is a candidate for a paid trial, but the cost calculator and a privacy review still apply. A high score does not override a failed privacy, legal, safety, or customer-impact check.

Limits of the scorecard

Seven equally weighted criteria are useful for a first comparison, but they are not universal truths. A support automation and an image generator have different failure costs. Change the weights only before testing, record the reason, and keep the same rubric across candidates.

What the score ignores

  • Vendor popularity
  • Affiliate payout
  • Number of features
  • Demo polish

Evidence rule

If a score is based on a vendor demo or another summary, say so. Do not label it as a first-hand test.
