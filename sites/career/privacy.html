Privacy and cookies

What this site processes, how advertising consent works and how to report a concern.

Hosting and basic access data

CareerSignal Lab is hosted by Vercel. The hosting provider may process standard technical information such as an IP address, browser type, requested URL and timestamps to deliver and protect the site.

Advertising

The site may use Google AdSense after approval. Google and its partners may process cookies or device information in accordance with applicable consent choices and legal bases. Consent defaults are set to deny advertising storage, advertising user data and ad personalization until the consent mechanism applicable to your region updates them.

Your choices

Use the “Privacy choices” button in the footer to open the available consent controls or Google's ad settings. You can also use browser controls to block or delete cookies, though some site functions may change.

External links and AI tools

Links to Rezi, Teal, OpenAI, Anthropic, Grammarly and other services lead to third-party sites with their own privacy policies. If you upload a resume or interview notes to a third-party service, review that service's retention and training settings first. This site does not receive the contents of those uploads.

Retention and contact

This static site does not provide user accounts or store resume files. For a privacy question or correction, use the contact page and avoid posting sensitive information publicly.

This page describes the current site configuration and is not legal advice. Privacy practices may change when hosting, advertising or analytics providers change.
