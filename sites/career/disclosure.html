Advertising disclosure

How commercial links and advertising are handled.

Current status

At the time of this review, CareerSignal Lab uses direct vendor links without affiliate tracking. If an affiliate relationship is added, the affected link will be disclosed before the first link and marked with rel="sponsored" where appropriate.

Editorial independence

Advertising and commercial relationships do not determine the stated strengths, limitations or recommendations of a product. A review may conclude that a free tool or a simpler workflow is the better choice.

Advertising labels

Advertising units are visually separated from editorial content and labeled. We do not place ads inside navigation, forms or screens that have no substantive publisher content.

Questions

If a disclosure is unclear, use the contact page and include the URL of the page you are reviewing.
