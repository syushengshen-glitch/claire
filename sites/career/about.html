About CareerSignal Lab

Practical, evidence-first guidance for using AI in a job search.

Why this site exists

CareerSignal Lab covers AI resume builders, job trackers, writing assistants and interview workflows for students, career changers and job seekers. The site is built around a simple rule: a tool should help you express real evidence more clearly, not manufacture a more impressive history.

Who maintains it

The site is maintained by an independent editorial project publishing under the CareerSignal Lab name. The public maintainer profile is available at github.com/syushengshen-glitch. We do not claim an employer relationship with any reviewed vendor, and we do not present a product as tested unless the page says what was tested and when.

How reviews are made

Reviews combine current official product information, documented pricing, clearly separated editorial judgment and a repeatable decision framework. We date price checks, link to sources, state limitations and avoid universal claims about ATS performance or interview outcomes.

Corrections and contact

Corrections are handled through the public issue tracker linked on the contact page. A useful correction includes the URL, the disputed sentence, the correct information and a current source.

Advertising

Advertising and editorial recommendations are kept separate. See the advertising disclosure for current commercial relationships and labeling rules.
