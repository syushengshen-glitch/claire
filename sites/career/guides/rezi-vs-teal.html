Rezi vs Teal for resume tailoring

Where each tool helps during a multi-application search.

Reviewed October 1, 2026 · CareerSignal Lab · Advertising is labeled separately

Choose based on whether you need formatting or application tracking. This guide uses the same shortlist criteria across every tool: the job it solves, the limits a buyer should verify, export and privacy considerations, and the total correction cost.

How to choose

Start with one real task and test every candidate using the same input. Record setup time, first usable result, correction time, cost, export limits, and the person responsible for approval.

Decision rule: keep a tool only when it improves the finished result after corrections.

The shortlist

Rezi

AI resume builder focused on job-description matching and ATS-friendly structure. Best for: Job seekers who want to tailor a resume to a specific posting.. Watch for: AI suggestions still need factual review.

Teal

Job-search platform with resume tailoring, tracking, and keyword feedback. Best for: Candidates managing several applications and versions of a resume.. Watch for: Not every suggestion is relevant.

ChatGPT

A flexible assistant for research, drafting, analysis, and everyday problem solving. Best for: People who want one general-purpose AI workspace for several kinds of work.. Watch for: Answers still need fact-checking.

Workflow

  1. Define the output and the reviewer.
  2. Give the tool real, non-sensitive examples.
  3. Verify facts, links, rights, and customer commitments.
  4. Publish only after a human editor approves the result.

Verdict

Choose based on whether you need formatting or application tracking. The best choice is the smallest stack that improves the final output without hiding the review step.
