Contact and corrections

Use the public issue tracker for factual corrections, broken links and privacy requests.

Corrections

Open a public issue at github.com/syushengshen-glitch/claire/issues/new. Include the page URL, the sentence that needs correction and a current source. Do not include passwords, private candidate information or confidential employer records.

Privacy and security

For a privacy or security report, use the security advisory form. Do not post sensitive information in a public issue.

What to expect

We review corrections against the cited source and update the page when the evidence supports a change. A correction may take time to appear in search results after publication.
