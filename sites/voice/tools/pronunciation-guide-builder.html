Pronunciation guide builder

Turn a pronunciation disagreement into a documented, testable delivery decision.

This template supports the guide How to build a pronunciation guide for names, brands, and acronyms. Use it as a working draft, then adapt the wording and fields to your own contracts, client process, and jurisdiction.

How to use this page

  1. Open the source guide and read the context first.
  2. Copy the template or run the calculator with one real, non-sensitive example.
  3. Check the output against the checklist below.
  4. Save the final version in your own document or project tool.

Checklist

  • Are all ambiguous words listed?
  • Is there a repeatable phonetic or audio cue?
  • Does one owner approve the final pronunciation?
  • Was the word tested inside a real sentence?
  • Is the guide stored with the project?

Example wording

Pronunciation entry

Written: Kalla

Phonetic cue: KAH-lah (first syllable stressed).

Reference: client recording 0:03–0:05.

Owner: brand lead.

Note: do not pronounce it like “caller.”

Do not skip

  • Relying on the writer’s guess.
  • Approving a pronunciation after the full record.
  • Using one entry for several language markets.
  • Forgetting to test the word at the final pace.
Scope note

This page is a production and rights template, not legal advice. Review consent, licensing, and disclosure wording with a qualified professional where the risk is material.
