Voice casting scorecard

A casting matrix that separates audience, emotion, authority, pace, and accent from personal taste.

This template supports the guide How to choose a voice for brand narration. Use it as a working draft, then adapt the wording and fields to your own contracts, client process, and jurisdiction.

How to use this page

  1. Open the source guide and read the context first.
  2. Copy the template or run the calculator with one real, non-sensitive example.
  3. Check the output against the checklist below.
  4. Save the final version in your own document or project tool.

Checklist

  • Is the listening context described?
  • Did I choose warmth or authority as the primary job?
  • Does the audition use real names and numbers?
  • Did I listen in the final playback environment?
  • Is the alternate and decision recorded?

Example wording

Casting matrix fields

Audience: operations managers listening on a laptop during work.

Emotional job: calm confidence, not hype.

Pace: measured, with clear pauses around numbers.

Trust cues: plain language, no exaggerated emphasis.

Test words: product name, three numbers, one acronym, one regional term.

Do not skip

  • Choosing a voice because it sounds impressive in a demo.
  • Ignoring accent and pronunciation until the final edit.
  • Using a fake dialect for authenticity.
  • Scoring voices without music, ambience, or the final visuals.
Scope note

This page is a production and rights template, not legal advice. Review consent, licensing, and disclosure wording with a qualified professional where the risk is material.
