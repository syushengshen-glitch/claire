Dubbing cost estimator

Budget translation, casting, recording, direction, editing, mixing, QC, and project management separately.

This template supports the guide How to estimate dubbing cost without forgetting the review work. Use it as a working draft, then adapt the wording and fields to your own contracts, client process, and jurisdiction.

How to use this page

  1. Open the source guide and read the context first.
  2. Copy the template or run the calculator with one real, non-sensitive example.
  3. Check the output against the checklist below.
  4. Save the final version in your own document or project tool.

Checklist

  • Is the runtime based on the approved cut?
  • Are translation and adaptation separate line items?
  • Are casting, direction, and pickups included?
  • Are editing, mixing, and native QC included?
  • Is there a revision buffer?

Example wording

Per-language budget fields

Approved runtime: 18 minutes.

Translation and adaptation: 1.5 days.

Casting and audition: 3 candidates.

Recording and direction: 3 hours.

Editing, mixing, QC: 1 day.

Revision buffer: 15%.

Do not skip

  • Quoting only voice recording minutes.
  • Using raw footage instead of the approved runtime.
  • Skipping native-language QC.
  • Forgetting music, captions, and loudness work.
Scope note

This page is a production and rights template, not legal advice. Review consent, licensing, and disclosure wording with a qualified professional where the risk is material.
