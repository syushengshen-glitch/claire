Consent, Rights & Disclosure

Licensed audio checklist

Check the license scope, media, territory, duration, edits, and platform before publishing.

This template supports the guide Licensed voice and music checklist for commercial audio. Use it as a working draft, then adapt the wording and fields to your own contracts, client process, and jurisdiction.

How to use this page

  1. Open the source guide and read the context first.
  2. Copy the template or run the calculator with one real, non-sensitive example.
  3. Check the output against the checklist below.
  4. Save the final version in your own document or project tool.

Checklist

  • Does the license cover the intended media and territory?
  • Are editing and synthetic-use rights clear?
  • Are music and sound effects licensed separately?
  • Are credit or disclosure requirements met?
  • Is the license archived with the final asset?

Example wording

License record fields

Asset name and creator.

License type and version.

Permitted media: paid social, no broadcast.

Territory: worldwide online.

Duration: 12 months.

Editing: allowed; model training prohibited.

Do not skip

  • Assuming a free download is free for commercial use.
  • Using a personal-use track in a paid ad.
  • Editing a voice sample beyond the license scope.
  • Failing to keep the receipt and license version.
Scope note

This page is a production and rights template, not legal advice. Review consent, licensing, and disclosure wording with a qualified professional where the risk is material.
