Loudness delivery checklist

A plain-language loudness workflow that avoids quiet exports, clipped peaks, and inconsistent episodes.

This template supports the guide Loudness for podcast and video: LUFS, peaks, and platform delivery. Use it as a working draft, then adapt the wording and fields to your own contracts, client process, and jurisdiction.

How to use this page

  1. Open the source guide and read the context first.
  2. Copy the template or run the calculator with one real, non-sensitive example.
  3. Check the output against the checklist below.
  4. Save the final version in your own document or project tool.

Checklist

  • Did I choose a platform-specific loudness target?
  • Did I measure integrated loudness and true peak?
  • Does the mix preserve useful dynamics?
  • Does speech remain intelligible under music?
  • Did I measure the final encoded file?

Example wording

Delivery note fields

Platform: client learning portal

Integrated loudness target: documented in the delivery spec

True peak limit: below clipping

Music level: reviewed under speech

QC: phone speaker, laptop, headphones

Do not skip

  • Normalizing peaks instead of measuring loudness.
  • Using one master for every platform.
  • Over-compressing until the voice is fatiguing.
  • Checking only in headphones.
Scope note

This page is a production and rights template, not legal advice. Review consent, licensing, and disclosure wording with a qualified professional where the risk is material.
