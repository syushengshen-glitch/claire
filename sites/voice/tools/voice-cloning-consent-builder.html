How to use this page

  1. Open the source guide and read the context first.
  2. Copy the template or run the calculator with one real, non-sensitive example.
  3. Check the output against the checklist below.
  4. Save the final version in your own document or project tool.

Checklist

  • Does the consent explicitly mention cloning and model training?
  • Are allowed and excluded uses listed?
  • Are territory, duration, and compensation clear?
  • Is there a review or revocation path?
  • Is access to the model and outputs controlled?

Example wording

Consent scope fields

Voice owner and date of consent.

Permitted projects: internal training videos only.

Excluded uses: political, adult, medical, financial, endorsements.

Territory and duration: Taiwan, 12 months.

Review date and revocation contact.

Access list for the model and generated files.

Do not skip

  • Treating a normal voiceover release as cloning consent.
  • Using an open-ended “all media forever” clause without discussion.
  • Leaving the model accessible to the whole organization indefinitely.
  • Assuming a synthetic voice cannot harm the person it resembles.
Scope note

This page is a production and rights template, not legal advice. Review consent, licensing, and disclosure wording with a qualified professional where the risk is material.
