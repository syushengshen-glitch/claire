How to use this page

  1. Open the source guide and read the context first.
  2. Copy the template or run the calculator with one real, non-sensitive example.
  3. Check the output against the checklist below.
  4. Save the final version in your own document or project tool.

Checklist

  • Are captions segmented by meaning?
  • Is reading speed comfortable on a phone?
  • Are speaker changes and meaningful sounds labeled?
  • Do captions avoid important visuals?
  • Did I watch the final captions in the target player?

Example wording

Caption defect examples

Line break splits “KAH-lah” across two lines.

Caption appears before the speaker starts by 1.5 seconds.

Two speakers share one caption with no labels.

Caption covers the product price in the lower third.

Do not skip

  • Publishing the raw transcript without timing.
  • Using character count as the only segmentation rule.
  • Ignoring speaker labels in interviews.
  • Checking captions only in the editing application.
