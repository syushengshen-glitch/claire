AI image editing rights checklist

Check model terms, source ownership, and disclosure before publishing.

Reviewed October 1, 2026 · ImageSignal Lab · Advertising is labeled separately

Document the rights and limitations behind every published visual. This guide uses the same shortlist criteria across every tool: the job it solves, the limits a buyer should verify, export and privacy considerations, and the total correction cost.

How to choose

Start with one real task and test every candidate using the same input. Record setup time, first usable result, correction time, cost, export limits, and the person responsible for approval.

Decision rule: keep a tool only when it improves the finished result after corrections.

The shortlist

Midjourney

High-quality image generation with a strong visual style and active community. Best for: Designers, concept artists, and creators who need polished visual exploration.. Watch for: No permanent free tier.

Leonardo AI

Image generation and editing platform with models for game, product, and concept art. Best for: Creators who want more control over models, styles, and production assets.. Watch for: Interface can feel dense for beginners.

Adobe Firefly

Adobe creative AI for image generation, editing, and design workflows. Best for: Adobe users who want generative features inside familiar creative tools.. Watch for: Best value is inside Adobe workflows.

Workflow

  1. Define the output and the reviewer.
  2. Give the tool real, non-sensitive examples.
  3. Verify facts, links, rights, and customer commitments.
  4. Publish only after a human editor approves the result.

Verdict

Document the rights and limitations behind every published visual. The best choice is the smallest stack that improves the final output without hiding the review step.
