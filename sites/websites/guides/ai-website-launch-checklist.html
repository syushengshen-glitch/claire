AI website launch checklist

Analytics, privacy, SEO, and ownership before launch.

Reviewed October 1, 2026 · WebSignal Lab · Advertising is labeled separately

Do not publish an AI-generated site without a review and maintenance plan. This guide uses the same shortlist criteria across every tool: the job it solves, the limits a buyer should verify, export and privacy considerations, and the total correction cost.

How to choose

Start with one real task and test every candidate using the same input. Record setup time, first usable result, correction time, cost, export limits, and the person responsible for approval.

Decision rule: keep a tool only when it improves the finished result after corrections.

The shortlist

Notion AI

AI assistance inside a connected workspace for notes, docs, projects, and databases. Best for: Teams that already organize work in Notion and want AI close to their knowledge base.. Watch for: Best value inside Notion.

Replit

Browser-based development environment with AI help, hosting, and collaboration. Best for: Beginners and small teams that want to build and deploy from a browser.. Watch for: Resource limits on lower plans.

Claude

A writing and reasoning assistant known for careful long-form responses. Best for: Writers, researchers, and teams working with long documents or nuanced instructions.. Watch for: Product availability can vary by region.

Workflow

  1. Define the output and the reviewer.
  2. Give the tool real, non-sensitive examples.
  3. Verify facts, links, rights, and customer commitments.
  4. Publish only after a human editor approves the result.

Verdict

Do not publish an AI-generated site without a review and maintenance plan. The best choice is the smallest stack that improves the final output without hiding the review step.
