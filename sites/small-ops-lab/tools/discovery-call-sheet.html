Discovery-call sheet

Ask better questions, disqualify bad fits early, and leave the call with a clear next step.

This template supports the guide A discovery-call script that exposes budget, deadline, and decision-maker. Use it as a working draft, then adapt the wording and fields to your own contracts, client process, and jurisdiction.

How to use this page

  1. Open the source guide and read the context first.
  2. Copy the template or run the calculator with one real, non-sensitive example.
  3. Check the output against the checklist below.
  4. Save the final version in your own document or project tool.

Checklist

  • Did I learn the current situation in their words?
  • Did I ask what happens if nothing changes?
  • Do I know the budget range and who approves it?
  • Is the deadline tied to a real event?
  • Did I confirm one specific next step and date?

Example wording

Call summary email

Hi Sam — thanks for the call. What I heard: the current intake form loses about 20% of requests, the new site must launch before the October campaign, and Maya approves the final budget. You asked for a fixed-scope proposal with two payment milestones. I will send a one-page scope by Thursday and include the two open questions we listed.

Next step: review the scope by Monday, then decide whether to book the build week.

Do not skip

  • Pitching for most of the call instead of asking questions.
  • Writing a detailed proposal before confirming budget and authority.
  • Accepting “sometime next quarter” as a deadline.
  • Ending without a written summary and a decision date.
Scope note

This page is an operational template, not legal, tax, or financial advice. Review the final wording with a qualified professional where the risk is material.
