How to use this page

  1. Open the source guide and read the context first.
  2. Copy the template or run the calculator with one real, non-sensitive example.
  3. Check the output against the checklist below.
  4. Save the final version in your own document or project tool.

Checklist

  • Can I describe the finished outcome in one sentence?
  • Have I listed what is excluded as clearly as what is included?
  • Is the revision limit and response time written down?
  • Are client responsibilities tied to dates?
  • Does every new request go through a change order?

Example wording

Scope boundary language

Included: five responsive pages, one content-editing session, two revision rounds, and a launch checklist.

Not included: copywriting, photography, paid ads, third-party subscription fees, and new page templates.

Client responsibilities: approve the sitemap within three business days and provide final copy before the build week.

Any request that changes the page count, audience, or approval path will be estimated separately before work starts.

Do not skip

  • Quoting a broad category instead of a finished deliverable.
  • Leaving revisions unlimited because the client seems reasonable.
  • Absorbing a change to avoid an awkward conversation.
  • Forgetting third-party costs, taxes, travel, or subscription fees.
Scope note

This page is an operational template, not legal, tax, or financial advice. Review the final wording with a qualified professional where the risk is material.
