Weekly status update generator

A short, repeatable update that makes progress, blockers, and decisions visible.

This template supports the guide Weekly client status update that reduces surprise meetings. Use it as a working draft, then adapt the wording and fields to your own contracts, client process, and jurisdiction.

How to use this page

  1. Open the source guide and read the context first.
  2. Copy the template or run the calculator with one real, non-sensitive example.
  3. Check the output against the checklist below.
  4. Save the final version in your own document or project tool.

Checklist

  • Does the first line state on track, at risk, or blocked?
  • Can the client verify the progress through a link or deliverable?
  • Does every blocker have an owner and a date?
  • Is the decision request specific and recommended?
  • Are budget and timeline visible?

Example wording

Weekly update template

Status: on track.

Done: sitemap approved; homepage draft ready for review.

Next: booking page build; need final copy by Wednesday.

Blockers: waiting on logo files (Sam, due Tuesday).

Decisions: choose booking provider A or B by Thursday — recommendation is A for lower setup cost.

Budget: 18 of 40 hours used; forecast remains 40 hours.

Do not skip

  • Sending an update only when something goes wrong.
  • Burying a blocker in the middle of a long message.
  • Asking for three decisions in one paragraph.
  • Reporting hours without a forecast.
Scope note

This page is an operational template, not legal, tax, or financial advice. Review the final wording with a qualified professional where the risk is material.
