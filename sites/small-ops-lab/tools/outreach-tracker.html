Warm outreach tracker

A warm outreach system built on specific problems, proof, and a low-friction next step.

This template supports the guide How to find your first five clients without cold-DM spam. Use it as a working draft, then adapt the wording and fields to your own contracts, client process, and jurisdiction.

How to use this page

  1. Open the source guide and read the context first.
  2. Copy the template or run the calculator with one real, non-sensitive example.
  3. Check the output against the checklist below.
  4. Save the final version in your own document or project tool.

Checklist

  • Can I point to one observable problem for this prospect?
  • Is the first step small enough to accept without a meeting?
  • Does the message state who I help and what I do?
  • Have I scheduled the two follow-ups before sending?
  • Am I tracking replies and outcomes, not just sends?

Example wording

A short first-touch message

Subject: Quick note about your booking page

Hi Maya — I noticed the booking page asks for a phone number but no preferred time window, which usually creates email ping-pong. I build small scheduling fixes for service businesses. If useful, I can send a two-minute outline of the change I would test first. No call needed to see it.

— Alex

Do not skip

  • Sending the same message to everyone and calling it personalization because the name changes.
  • Leading with your life story instead of the prospect problem.
  • Asking for a 30-minute call before offering any value.
  • Following up forever instead of closing the loop after three touches.
Scope note

This page is an operational template, not legal, tax, or financial advice. Review the final wording with a qualified professional where the risk is material.
