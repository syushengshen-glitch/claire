Referral email generator

Turn a happy client into one warm introduction without making the request awkward.

This template supports the guide The referral request email that asks for a specific introduction. Use it as a working draft, then adapt the wording and fields to your own contracts, client process, and jurisdiction.

How to use this page

  1. Open the source guide and read the context first.
  2. Copy the template or run the calculator with one real, non-sensitive example.
  3. Check the output against the checklist below.
  4. Save the final version in your own document or project tool.

Checklist

  • Am I asking after a visible result?
  • Did I name one specific type of client?
  • Is the forwardable message short enough to copy?
  • Did I make it easy to decline?
  • Will I report back on the outcome?

Example wording

Forwardable referral paragraph

I work with small service businesses that lose leads because their intake process depends on phone tag. I map the current flow, fix the highest-friction step, and leave a documented process the team can run. If that sounds relevant, the first step is a short call to check fit.

No pressure either way — I appreciate the work we did together regardless.

Do not skip

  • Asking for a referral before delivering a result.
  • Offering a vague category instead of a specific person.
  • Asking the client to write the introduction from scratch.
  • Following up repeatedly after a clear no.
Scope note

This page is an operational template, not legal, tax, or financial advice. Review the final wording with a qualified professional where the risk is material.
