How to use this page

  1. Open the source guide and read the context first.
  2. Copy the template or run the calculator with one real, non-sensitive example.
  3. Check the output against the checklist below.
  4. Save the final version in your own document or project tool.

Checklist

  • Does the deposit cover the work before the first milestone?
  • Is every payment tied to a visible deliverable?
  • Is the final payment collected before final handoff?
  • Are paused projects and unused hours addressed?
  • Is the schedule in both the quote and the contract?

Example wording

Three-payment schedule

40% deposit — reserves the build week and covers discovery.

30% at approved design direction — starts production.

30% before final handoff — releases source files, access, and launch support.

Do not skip

  • Starting work with no deposit because the client seems friendly.
  • Tying payments to calendar dates that do not match progress.
  • Delivering all files before the final payment clears.
  • Changing the schedule mid-project without updating the agreement.
Scope note

This page is an operational template, not legal, tax, or financial advice. Review the final wording with a qualified professional where the risk is material.
