Project handoff checklist

A clean closeout protects access, payment, future work, and your reputation.

This template supports the guide Project handoff and closeout: what to deliver and what to keep. Use it as a working draft, then adapt the wording and fields to your own contracts, client process, and jurisdiction.

How to use this page

  1. Open the source guide and read the context first.
  2. Copy the template or run the calculator with one real, non-sensitive example.
  3. Check the output against the checklist below.
  4. Save the final version in your own document or project tool.

Checklist

  • Is the final payment confirmed?
  • Are files delivered in an openable, agreed format?
  • Was access transferred without plain-text credentials?
  • Is the support window written down?
  • Did I ask for feedback and a specific next step?

Example wording

Closeout email skeleton

The site is live and the final invoice is paid. Attached: source files, export, credentials transfer note, and a two-page handoff guide.

Support: bug fixes are covered for 14 days. New features or content changes can be quoted separately.

If you have five minutes, a short testimonial would help. If a specific person comes to mind who needs the same fix, I am happy to send a one-paragraph introduction.

Do not skip

  • Releasing all files before final payment clears.
  • Sending passwords in an email or chat message.
  • Leaving support expectations undefined.
  • Ending the project with no feedback or future-work conversation.
Scope note

This page is an operational template, not legal, tax, or financial advice. Review the final wording with a qualified professional where the risk is material.
